## Iteration 2

Everything from iteration 1, plus three changes:

- **Double down**: on your first two cards you can double. That means exactly one more card, then you stand, on a doubled stake. The odds table shows DOUBLE with its win/push/lose chances and EV.
- **The dealer peeks**: when the dealer shows an ace or a ten, they check the hole card for blackjack before you act, and the hand ends there if they have it. The odds are now worked out knowing the hole card is not the one that makes blackjack.
- **A bankroll**: you start with $100 and every hand is a flat $1 bet (a double makes it $2). The balance carries across hands until you quit or run out of money.

Still not in: no splitting, surrender, insurance or variable bet sizing, and blackjack still pays 1:1 (3:2 not yet).
